# Legal and policy review checklist

This checklist captures the handoff required before a legal policy becomes
public. It is not legal advice and does not mean a draft policy has been
approved.

## Required review package

- [ ] Assign counsel or the responsible compliance owner and record the
      governing entity, jurisdiction, effective date, review date, and version.
- [ ] Review the English source and all published translations for semantic
      equivalence. Do not publish machine-translated legal text without the
      required review.
- [ ] Confirm the public routes and footer links for: Terms of Service,
      Privacy Policy, Research Use Only Policy, Shipping & Compliance Notice,
      and Cookie Policy.
- [ ] Confirm the CMS legal page is `PUBLISHED`, marked `APPROVED`, and has a
      recorded legal-review timestamp. Pending or unapproved legal pages must
      remain unavailable to the public and absent from the sitemap.

## Privacy and cookie checks

- [ ] Privacy Policy identifies controller/contact details, categories and
      purposes of inquiry data, lawful basis, retention, processors/transfers,
      security measures, subject rights, and the contact/escalation route
      required for the target markets (including GDPR where applicable).
- [ ] Cookie Policy lists necessary and optional analytics categories, vendors,
      purposes, duration, and how visitors change their choices.
- [ ] Verify first visit shows the Cookie choices banner, Reject all works,
      analytics remains off unless opted in, and Cookie settings supports later
      withdrawal.
- [ ] Confirm inquiry attachment handling is private, staff downloads require
      authorization, downloads are audited, and anonymous attachment access is
      denied.

## B2B, scientific, shipping, and publication review

- [ ] Terms identify acceptable site use, intellectual-property ownership,
      B2B contact process, limitation language, and governing law/forum as
      advised by counsel.
- [ ] RUO Policy clearly limits products and discussions to qualified,
      compliant research use; rejects sales to unqualified individuals and
      minors where applicable; and avoids medical-use claims.
- [ ] Shipping & Compliance Notice accurately states supported carriers (only
      where actually offered), Incoterms/customs responsibility, export/sanctions
      screening, destination restrictions, and buyer responsibilities.
- [ ] Counsel approves the final content and effective date in writing before
      publication. Store that approval outside the public repository.
